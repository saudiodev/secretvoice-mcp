## Copy to paste

**Name:** SecretVoice

**Tagline (60):** Intimate voice AI with emoji vocal cues and multi-voice scenes

**Short (160):** SecretVoice is voice AI tuned for closeness — 29 voices, 22 languages,
emoji vocal cues that direct whisper and sigh, and multi-voice scenes over OAuth 2.1.

**Long (400):** SecretVoice is not a generic text-to-speech wrapper. It is voice AI tuned
for closeness: close-mic whispers, breathy delivery and chest-voice warmth instead of
boardroom narration. Direction lives in the script — drop 👂 for whisper, 😮‍💨 for a sigh,
🌬️ for breathless, 💋 for a kiss — and the engine performs it. `generate_scene` renders a
multi-voice scene with a different voice and language per segment, so dialogue and
audiobook chapters come out in one call. 29 voices, 22 languages, OAuth 2.1 with dynamic
client registration, and a free 3-day trial.
